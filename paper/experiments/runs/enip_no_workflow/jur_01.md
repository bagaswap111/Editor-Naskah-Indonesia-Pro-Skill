### 3. Catatan Editor (Perubahan Signifikan)

*   **Kosakata Baku:** "sistim" $\rightarrow$ "sistem", "ijin" $\rightarrow$ "izin", "online" $\rightarrow$ "daring", "kwalitas" $\rightarrow$ "kualitas".
